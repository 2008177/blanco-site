What Blanco is

Blanco is a personal assistant built and used by one person, its developer, for their own day-to-day organisation. It is not a public product: nobody else can sign up or use it.

What it does

  • Every morning it sends its owner a short brief, in their own private Telegram chat, covering today's calendar, emails that may need a reply, and open tasks.
  • It answers its owner's questions about their day, such as "what's at 3pm?".
  • It adds tasks to, and completes tasks in, the owner's Google Tasks when the owner asks it to.

Google data it uses

When its owner signs in with Google, Blanco asks for:

  • Gmail, read-only: to read recent emails for the morning brief. It never sends, deletes, archives or changes email.
  • Google Calendar, read-only: to list the day's events.
  • Google Tasks: to list open tasks, and to add or complete tasks when the owner asks.
